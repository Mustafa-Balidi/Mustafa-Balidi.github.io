Biomedical IR System

Academic project · NLP & information retrieval

An end-to-end retrieval system over a biomedical corpus: an NLTK preprocessing pipeline, TF-IDF vector space and BM25 inverted index baselines, then semantic retrieval with GloVe and BERT embeddings and RM3 query expansion — all compared on MAP, nDCG and precision at 10.
